## Coaching Notes

- Full course is 3.27 miles — long lap, consistency critical
- Back straight (T7 to T8) is one of the longest on the calendar — big braking zone at T8
- South Bend / Oak Tree section (T10-T11) is fast and flowing — rhythm matters
- Roller Coaster (T14) is the signature corner — blind crest into braking
- Hog Pen (T17) defines your lap time — good exit = fast S/F straight
