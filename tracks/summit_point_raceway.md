## Coaching Notes

- Short track (under 2 miles) — lap times under 1:30 typical, consistency is everything
- T7 is likely the tightest corner — heavy braking zone
- Lots of right-handers (T1, T4, T6, T7, T8, T9, T10) — left-side tires will work hard
- Track is relatively flat compared to VIR or Road America
- Free track — good for unlimited practice sessions
