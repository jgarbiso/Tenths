## Coaching Notes

- Horse Shoe (T1) and Hog Pen (T17) are the defining corners — both tight and require patience
- The Snake (T6) section is technical — smooth steering inputs critical
- Roller Coaster (T14) has significant elevation change — brake on the crest, not after
- NASCAR Bend (T3) area transitions into the infield — carry speed through
- Track flows well once rhythm is found — consistency is key on this config
