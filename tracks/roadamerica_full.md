## Coaching Notes

- T1 and T12 (Canada Corner) are the two heaviest braking zones — long straights into hard stops
- The Kink (T11) is reputation-defining — a fast left that's near-flat in many cars but punishes mistakes severely
- Carousel (T10) is a long-duration corner — patience on throttle application
- The track is 4+ miles so each lap is a test of consistency — variance adds up fast
- Elevation changes throughout — downhill into T1, uphill through T5-T6
