## Coaching Notes

- T5 (the chicane at 83%) is the biggest opportunity — you proved you can carry 106mph through it on your best lap. Most laps you over-brake to 77-87mph. Pick a fixed brake marker and commit.
- T2 has oversteer risk — release brake before rotation to avoid snapping the rear.
- T4 is the heaviest stop on track (136→53mph on some laps) — ABS management critical here.
- The Esses (T7-T8) are commitment corners — flat or near-flat in the GT3. Confidence builds with laps.
- ABS improved massively across sessions: 124 → 70 → 36 → 30 average. Brake modulation is progressing well.
- Best laps coming late in sessions — still finding time. Focus next session on nailing T5 from lap 1.
