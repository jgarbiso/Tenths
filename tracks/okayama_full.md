## Coaching Notes

- T1 First Corner: Heavy ABS with oscillating inputs — progressive brake application, release before turn-in.
- T9 Hobbs: Heaviest stop on track. Build pressure gradually, not stab-and-hold.
- T5 Hair Pin: Second-heaviest stop. Same technique focus as T1.
- T10 Mike Knight: Oversteer risk — release brake fully before committing to rotation.
- ABS worsening late in sessions (+300 from first to second half) — pace yourself, maintain focus on brake modulation.
- Only 2 valid laps analyzed — more data will refine these observations.
