## Coaching Notes

- The Corkscrew (T8) is the signature corner — brake BEFORE the crest, not after. Trust the car will rotate as it drops.
- Andretti Hairpin (T2) is the tightest point — patience on throttle, don't rush the exit
- Rahal Straight into T6 is the biggest stop — longest braking zone on track
- Elevation changes throughout — the track drops 5.5 stories through the Corkscrew alone
- T5 uphill braking is tricky — car is light under braking on the crest
- Track not yet licensed — "Add to Cart" shown in iRacing
